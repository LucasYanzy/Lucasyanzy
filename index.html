HackRU26 · Team project · Oct 2026
Three.js TypeScript Node.js Solana Presage
My role / 我的职责
Weather and map system development · Modeling and the upgrade system · Core gameplay design and planning天气与地图系统开发 · 建模与升级系统 · 玩法核心设计与策划
A browser game you play by holding your real breath. A webcam, read through Presage's vital-signs SDK, detects the breath-hold: the diver only descends while you hold it, deeper water gives rarer fish, and breathing underwater means racing back to the surface or losing the catch. Fish can be minted as Solana NFTs. 用真实憋气来玩的浏览器游戏。摄像头通过 Presage 检测是否在憋气：憋气时潜水员才会下潜，越深的水域鱼越稀有；水下一呼吸就得赶紧回到水面，否则网里的鱼全部丢失。收获的鱼可以铸造成 Solana NFT。
